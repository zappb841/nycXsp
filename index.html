How many passengers are riding the subway per square meter?


During peak hours, there are approxmatly 5 people per square meter
in New York City's subways, while in São Paulo that number goes up to 9.

Subway Fare x Minimum Wage Over Time


In São Paulo, a worker that earns the minumum wage needs to work
for 44 minutes to pay for a subway ride. In New York, they need to work for 11 minutes.
